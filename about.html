The people and the plan behind the plants

The Solar Land connects investors with carefully selected solar projects. We build and run the plants, and our investors earn a return from the energy they produce.

What we do

We organize turn-key solar projects. We find the site, handle permits, build the plant and run it for at least 25 years.

See how investing works

  • Find the site

    Strong sunlight and a good grid connection.

  • Handle permits

    Planning and approvals before construction.

  • Build the plant

    Design, construction and testing, in up to 24 months.

  • Run it for 25+ years

    Monitoring, maintenance and selling the electricity.
