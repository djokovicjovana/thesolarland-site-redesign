The investment

What is The Solar Land?

An investment platform for solar energy. You invest, we build and run the plants, and you earn a return from the electricity they sell.

What am I buying?

A fixed share of the plants’ energy, measured in MWh a year and set in your contract for 25 years.

Who can invest?

People from many countries, including first-time investors. Some legal limits may apply where you live.

What is the minimum investment?

It depends on the plan. Our team will confirm the current minimum.

Returns

How much can I earn?

A contractual return of 4.33% a year, with an estimated 6.5% a year that depends on electricity sales. The calculator shows your own numbers.

When do I get paid?

Returns start within 2 years of your investment, while the plant is built. Our Solarverse community may offer ways to start earlier.

How are returns paid?

On the schedule in your contract, directly to your chosen account, with regular reports.

Can I reinvest my returns?

Yes. Reinvested returns earn returns too, so your income grows over time.

Risk and flexibility

What if a plant produces less than expected?

Your share stays the same, because the contract puts this risk on The Solar Land. The plants are also insured, monitored and maintained every year. What your contract covers

Can I withdraw early?

Yes, without penalties, but it may take some time to arrange. You can also transfer your investment to another investor, with our approval.

Are there tax benefits?

It depends on your country. We don’t give tax advice, so please ask a tax advisor.

The plants

Can I visit a plant?

Yes. We organize investor visits every year and when each plant opens.
