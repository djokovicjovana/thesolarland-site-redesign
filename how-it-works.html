From your investment to your first payout

You invest once. We build the plant, run it and sell its electricity. You receive your return on the schedule in your contract.

  1. Your investment
  2. Solar plant
  3. Electricity sold
  4. Your return
  1. 1

    Register

    Create your investor account, and our team reviews your application. Once approved, you join Solarverse, our investor community. Members get extra benefits, such as offers to start receiving returns sooner.

  2. 2

    Invest

    Choose your amount and sign your contract. It sets your share of energy, in MWh a year, and your return for 25 years. You pay securely online and get your confirmation and documents right away.

  3. 3

    We build

    We choose a site with strong sunlight and a good grid connection, then design, build and test the plant. This takes up to 24 months.

  4. 4

    You get paid

    The plant feeds its electricity into the grid, and we sell it under long-term contracts. You receive your return and regular production reports. You can withdraw your returns or reinvest them.

What your contract covers

Every investor has a contract. Here is what it covers, in plain words.

What is fixed
Your share of energy (MWh a year) and your contractual return, for 25 years.
Two numbers
4.33%Contractual return a year. This is the guaranteed part.
6.5%Estimated return a year. It depends on the electricity sales contracts we secure, so it is not fixed.
If a plant produces less
Your share stays the same. The contract puts this risk on The Solar Land, not on you.
Who stands behind it
The Solar Land, through your contract. Your share is always a small part of our total production.
What protects the plants
Insurance with reputable insurers, continuous monitoring, yearly maintenance and long-term electricity contracts.

The exact terms are in your investment contract. Please read it carefully, and talk to a financial or legal advisor if anything is unclear.
